- `TUI/AI-Project-Manager/` — PowerShell Operator Interface.

- `Tests/` — Automated Regression, Security, And Packaging Tests.
- `UX-TUI/` — Brand Assets, Theme, And UI Design References.
- `docs/guides/` — Beginner Guides.
- `docs/project/` — Maintained Architecture, Governance, Provenance, Support, And Release Documentation.
