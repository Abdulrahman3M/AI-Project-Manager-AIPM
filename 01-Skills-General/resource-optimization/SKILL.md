# Resource Optimization

Project Manager activates this policy alongside Task Scope Contract for every AIPM-managed interaction, including simple, active, resumed, revised, reopened, fix and recheck work. It remains active without repeatedly loading full instructions. Correctness, owner requirements, safety, governance and required tests outrank efficiency.

## Token Optimization

Read indexes and authoritative candidates selectively, then necessary ranges and justified dependencies. Reuse unchanged verified context; refresh changed sources. Keep compact, lossless requirements, decisions, evidence references, blockers and checkpoints instead of repeating history or logs. Reason proportionally and avoid re-exploring rejected options. Preserve every constraint, negative requirement, acceptance criterion, data integrity rule and required test. Never claim unmeasured savings.

For analysis, preserve verified evidence, competing causes and relevant tradeoffs. An execution-ready handoff for another agent belongs to `create-strict-implementation-handoff` and must remain complete; its creation is not permission to implement. During authorized execution use bounded edits, actual verification and resumable milestones. Direct owner Skill selection and applicable project governance remain authoritative.

## Time Efficiency

Sequence dependent steps, batch independent reads where safe, avoid repetitive analysis, and resume at the first unverified step. Choose the smallest adequate specialist set. Do not sacrifice quality or necessary checks to save time.

## Usage Limit Efficiency

Track input, output, cached tokens and provider quotas only when the host supplies reliable measurements. Otherwise report `NOT_MEASURED`; label non-authoritative estimates `ESTIMATE`. Never invent rolling-window usage, quota depletion or numerical savings. Reconcile limits and checkpoints on resume or revision.

## Tool Efficiency

Use targeted discovery and focused output; avoid repeated full reads and unnecessary tool calls. Task Scope Contract owns read tracking, scope expansion and bounded retry; Resource Optimization records their time and call cost. Independent lookups may be batched without creating additional agents.

## Task Resource Budget

Adapt targets and observations for tokens, calls, elapsed time, limits, risk, dependencies, milestones and required verification to task size and complexity. Reassess changed requirements or resumed work without erasing prior evidence. A target is not a fabricated provider allowance and never makes a required test optional. Compare equivalent measured fixtures where available; otherwise identify missing telemetry honestly.

## Sub-Agent Governance

`SMALL`: never spawn. `MEDIUM`: one agent by default, with no automatic spawning. `HUGE`: first establish genuinely independent work and material benefit, then ask the owner for explicit approval before spawning. The request must describe roles, exact file boundaries, collision avoidance, count, token/limit overhead, verification and the sequential alternative. No answer or refusal means serial execution; do not re-ask without a material change. If approved, provide scoped packets and budgets, avoid competing writes and verify results. An approval to develop AIPM is not approval to delegate.

## Reporting/Evidence

Report actual measured calls, time and tokens only where reliably available, and distinguish measurements from estimates and `NOT_MEASURED`. List meaningful remaining limits and required checks. Never claim that the [decision checker](scripts/resource_policy.py) launches agents, queries a provider or enforces a host's tools; it only assesses supplied records.
