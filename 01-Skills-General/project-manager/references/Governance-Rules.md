# Governance Rules

Read the current authority and relevant specialist rules first. Classify candidate rules as universal, conditional, project-specific, technical, or historical; deduplicate by intent without losing exceptions. Resolve conflicts by owner and project authority, scope and current evidence. Generalize behavior only when it survives a change of project, technology, paths and language. Keep root governance, narrowly scoped specialist rules, procedures and historical records distinct.

For create, merge, audit or evolve work, inventory sources, apply the universalization test, use the retained governance method and template, and verify rule preservation, links, localized copies, provenance, licensing, compatibility, rollback, security, evidence and versioning. Naming is project policy; preserve technical names. A permanent rule belongs at the correct authority level and must be synchronized when required. Do not silently resolve material policy choices that require the owner.

See `governance/` for the preserved rule catalog, synthesis methods and source matrix, and `../templates/AGENTS-Template.md` for the adaptable template.
