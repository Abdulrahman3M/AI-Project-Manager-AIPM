# Academic and Professional Document Modes

Route structure by document purpose instead of forcing one universal outline.

## Supported modes

- Research Paper
- Empirical Study Report
- Literature Review
- Systematic/Structured Review (only when methodology actually supports the label)
- Review Article
- Thesis/Dissertation Chapter
- Technical Report
- White Paper
- Policy Brief
- Case Study
- Educational Article/Chapter
- Professional Report
- Journal Manuscript
- Book/Long-form Chapter

Each mode defines required/optional front matter, section order, evidence density, citation expectations and conclusion behavior.

## Requirements and examples

- REQ-AD-01 — document mode is selected before outline generation.
- REQ-AD-02 — section grammar follows the chosen mode.
- REQ-AD-03 — labels such as “systematic review” require matching method.
- REQ-AD-04 — the mode may be customized by explicit owner/journal rules.
