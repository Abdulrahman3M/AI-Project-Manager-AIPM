# Tables and Figures Intelligence

Choose tables and figures by information shape and publication needs.

## Tables

Use native Word tables for structured comparison/data. Support repeated header rows, notes, units, sources, continuation behavior and landscape routing. Split a table only when readability improves and preserve semantic relation.

## Figures

Use the Visual Explanation System. Every figure has stable ID, caption, source/provenance, alt text and list-of-figures behavior where required. Charts retain the underlying data artifact.

## Requirements and examples

- REQ-TI-01 — table/figure choice follows information shape.
- REQ-TI-02 — quantitative visuals retain source data and units.
- REQ-TI-03 — oversized tables are routed to landscape/appendix or sensibly split.
- REQ-TI-04 — captions, numbering and cross-references remain stable after edits.
