# Literature Review and Synthesis Engine

A literature review synthesizes evidence across sources; it must not become a sequence of one-source summaries.

## Synthesis workflow

1. Define review question and inclusion boundary.
2. Normalize terminology and outcome definitions.
3. Build study/source comparison records.
4. Cluster evidence by themes, methods, populations or competing explanations.
5. Identify agreement, disagreement, strength, limitations and gaps.
6. Draft synthesis paragraphs that cite multiple relevant sources where appropriate.
7. Separate source findings from the document author's inference.
8. Maintain a Literature Synthesis Table as reproducibility evidence.

## Requirements and examples

- REQ-LR-01 — review question and inclusion boundary are explicit.
- REQ-LR-02 — synthesis groups evidence by meaningful dimensions.
- REQ-LR-03 — source-by-source serial summaries are not accepted as final synthesis.
- REQ-LR-04 — gaps and contradictions are linked back to evidence records.
