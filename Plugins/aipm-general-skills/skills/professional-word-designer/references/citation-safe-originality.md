# Citation-Safe Originality Review

This is an attribution and paraphrase-safety check, not a plagiarism-detector guarantee.

## Review

- Identify long or distinctive wording that appears too close to a consulted source.
- Convert unnecessary quotation-like paraphrase into genuine synthesis.
- Keep exact quotes exact, short, attributed and located when available.
- Preserve technical phrases that cannot reasonably be paraphrased.
- Check that paraphrased ideas still carry citation where attribution is needed.
- Never claim a percentage of plagiarism or guaranteed detector outcome without a real authorized detector.

## Requirements and examples

- REQ-OR-01 — distinctive borrowed language is quoted or genuinely paraphrased.
- REQ-OR-02 — paraphrase does not remove required attribution.
- REQ-OR-03 — exact quotations preserve wording and locator when available.
- REQ-OR-04 — no unsupported plagiarism/AI-detection score is fabricated.
