# Editorial Consistency Analyzer

Run a consistency pass across the complete document or chapter set.

Check terminology, capitalization, names, abbreviations, units, dates, numerical precision, cross-references, definitions, heading parallelism, duplicate passages, repeated explanations, contradictory statements and figure/table terminology.

Do not automatically rewrite a deliberate variation simply to force uniformity.

## Requirements and examples

- REQ-EC-01 — term variants are normalized or deliberately documented.
- REQ-EC-02 — duplicated substantive passages are flagged.
- REQ-EC-03 — contradictory internal statements are resolved or disclosed.
- REQ-EC-04 — consistency fixes preserve meaning and source support.
