# Claim–Evidence Matrix and Source Quality

Every material factual claim must be traceable to one or more consulted source records. Claim IDs remain stable across outline, draft, revision and QA. This is an editorial control, not a machine-guaranteed truth detector.

## Claim record and status

Record claim_id, claim text, type, section, supporting and contradicting source IDs, exact locator, scope/date/population, evidence status, qualification, draft disposition and review state.

Statuses: supported; qualified; contested; unsupported; inference; or not applicable with reason. An unsupported central claim blocks release until removed, narrowed or transparently marked. A bibliography entry alone does not prove that it supports a sentence.

**Example.** C-014: “In the studied cities, greater canopy was associated with lower daytime surface temperature.” Support S004/Table 2 and S011/Figure 3; retain geographic limit and the word “associated.” This example is illustrative, not a real finding.

## Source classification and appraisal

Tag sources as primary research, systematic review, narrative review, official record, standard, dataset, book, news, commentary, reference work or user-supplied. Primary does not automatically mean high quality. Assess authority, directness, method quality, relevance, recency, independence, integrity and access level with concise reasons.

**Example.** An official regulator is authoritative for its own current regulation but does not independently establish a scientific causal claim. Choose source type relative to the claim.

Do not calculate a universal certainty score unless a validated domain method is specified and actually applied. For conflicting findings, compare definitions, populations, periods, measures, methods and source dependencies; do not average incompatible results.

**Example.** Different definitions of heat exposure may explain conflicting studies. If evidence cannot resolve the reason, preserve the disagreement in the text.

## Acceptance checks

Every central question has mapped evidence or a documented gap. Every material factual claim has a claim ID or a recorded exemption. Every source ID exists. Key quotations and numbers have exact locators. Qualified and contested claims retain their limitations in the prose.

**Example.** An unsourced statistic is removed or labeled unverified; a plausible citation is never invented to fill the gap.
