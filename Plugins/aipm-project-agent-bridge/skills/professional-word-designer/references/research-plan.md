# Research Plan

The Research Plan operationalizes the Document Contract before source collection.

Record research questions, subquestions, scope, time range, jurisdictions/populations, inclusion criteria, exclusion criteria, preferred source classes, freshness rule, search channels/tools, stopping criteria and known access constraints.

## Requirements and examples

- REQ-PL-01 — each research question maps to planned evidence.
- REQ-PL-02 — inclusion/exclusion criteria are written before broad synthesis.
- REQ-PL-03 — stopping criteria are explicit.
- REQ-PL-04 — tool/access limitations are recorded before conclusions rely on unavailable evidence.
